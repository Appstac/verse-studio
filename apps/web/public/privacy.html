Privacy policy

Verse Studio · last updated 11 October 2026

Verse Studio is a personal tool that makes Bible-verse videos and edits video clips for one Instagram account. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When you sign in with Google, Verse Studio asks for your email address and access to files in Google Drive that Verse Studio itself creates (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Where your data is kept

Uploaded clips, settings and finished videos are stored only in the “Verse Studio” folder in your own Google Drive. This website has no database or server of its own and keeps nothing after you close it. Videos are processed on the account owner's own computer.

Who it is shared with

Your data is not sold or shared. To plan captions and edits, text such as transcripts and still frames from your clips is sent to Anthropic's Claude service under the account owner's own Claude subscription. Verse text is looked up from the public bible-api.com service.

Removing your data

Delete the “Verse Studio” folder from Google Drive, and remove Verse Studio's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
